## About GeoBridge

GeoBridge was a collaborative initiative connecting researchers and students using GIS and remote sensing to study landscape dynamics and environmental change, bringing together the University of Bergen and collaborators at the University of Amsterdam.

The initiative ran from **March 2025 to July 2026**. Its activities, team, and projects are documented on the **[GeoBridge website](https://mountains-in-motion.github.io/geobridge/)**.
